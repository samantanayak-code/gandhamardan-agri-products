# Gandhamardan Agri Products Chatbot

## Overview
This is a custom chatbot developed for Gandhamardan Agri Products, an organic agriculture and agri-processing enterprise. The chatbot serves as a virtual assistant to provide customers with accurate information about products, services, and business practices while directing commercial inquiries to the appropriate contact channels.

## Features

### Product Information
- Detailed information about rice & paddy products (organic black, red, white rice varieties)
- Millet products including packaged snacks, namkeen, murukku, and ready-to-eat cookies
- NutriBloom nutrition products including super protein powder
- Traditional herbs (48+ Ayurvedic herbs in raw and powder form)

### Intelligent Responses
- Natural language processing for common queries
- Context-aware responses based on product categories
- Automatic detection of pricing/commercial inquiries

### Business Compliance
- Strictly follows the policy of not sharing pricing information
- Directs commercial inquiries to the "Contact Us" section
- Prevents unauthorized medical claims about products

## Usage Instructions

1. Open the `chatbot.html` file in any web browser
2. Type your questions about products, services, or company information
3. The chatbot will respond with relevant information
4. For pricing, bulk orders, or commercial inquiries, you'll be directed to the Contact Us section

## System Instructions Implemented

The chatbot follows these key guidelines:
- Provides accurate product information without fabrication
- Explains product categories and traditional benefits
- Directs all pricing and commercial discussions to the Contact Us section
- Does not make unauthorized medical claims
- Maintains professional and informative tone
- Aligns with organic, traditional, and ethical values

## Supported Queries

The chatbot understands and responds to queries about:
- Rice varieties (black, red, white rice types)
- Millet products and snacks
- NutriBloom nutrition supplements
- Traditional herbs (Ashwagandha, Triphala, Amla, Brahmi, Guduchi, Mulethi, etc.)
- Company overview and business practices
- Contact information and ordering process
- Bulk orders and wholesale supply (directed to Contact Us)

## Technical Details

- Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript implementation (no external dependencies)
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices
- Simulated typing indicators for realistic interaction
- Built-in product knowledge base
- Clean and intuitive user interface with brand colors

## Integration

To integrate with the main website, simply:
1. Copy the chatbot JavaScript code into your existing page
2. Adjust the styling to match your website's CSS
3. Modify the product information as needed
4. Ensure the Contact Us redirection works with your site structure
